# Repository principles

- Do not add AI or tool attribution to branch names, commits, pull requests,
  comments, or documentation. Avoid names such as `codex/branch-name`, generated-by
  notices, and AI co-author trailers.
- Keep comments simple and to the point. Explain intent or constraints when the
  code alone is not clear.
- Keep `README.md` focused on information useful to users, such as requirements,
  installation, configuration, usage, and limitations. Put internal design and
  contributor details in `ARCHITECTURE.md`.
- Read `ARCHITECTURE.md` before changing the driver. Update it as needed when
  structure, responsibilities, data flow, or architectural constraints change.
